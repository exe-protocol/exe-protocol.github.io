How Exe Protocol works

Signal in. Settlement out.

Your XPR makes fees cheaper

XPR, your Exe Protocol Rate, functions as your personalized ‘exchange rate.’ XPR is designed to carry across every app that uses Exe Protocol. Earned through verified on-chain signal, higher XPR means lower fees from 0–100% determined by your activity.

Shared pool

Your XPR determines how much of your fee is redeemed from Exe’s pool. The higher your XPR, the more fee redemption you get under policy. A user never gets back more than the fees they paid.

Settle later

Each payment is approved and recorded instantly, then settled on-chain later in netted batches. Fewer settlements mean lower costs than typical on-chain transactions that settle every payment individually.

Built for agents

Designed so an agent can pay on its own, instantly — no human, no crypto housekeeping. Set the budget and limits up front. Exe Protocol pays the gas and clears every payment within those limits.
