How Exe Protocol works

Signal in. Settlement out.

XPR, your exchange rate

Earned through verified on-chain activity, and designed to carry across every app that uses Exe Protocol. Higher XPR, more of your execution cost paid by your activity: at Tier 1 with the pool at full strength, XPR 0.20 pays 20%, XPR 1.00 pays 100%.

Pool strength

Your rate is the same wherever you go, but your money buys less in an expensive city. Each pool shows its strength, and fee redemption never covers more than a trade's fees.

Settle later

Payments are recorded the moment they are approved, then settled on-chain in batches, using a fraction of the transactions.

Built for agents

Designed so agents need no approval at payment time and hold no gas token: budgets are funded in advance, the paymaster covers gas, and each payment is approved automatically within set limits.
