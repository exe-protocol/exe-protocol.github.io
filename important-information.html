What this site is

This site shows a working prototype of Exe Protocol and Exeswap, the first app built on Exe Protocol. Nothing on it is live: no wallet connects, no trade is executed and no real money moves.

Every figure is simulated

Every figure on this site comes from Exe Protocol’s simulation model unless it is labelled measured (timed in the prototype) or sourced (from a linked public source). Simulated figures are not quotes, forecasts or promises of any user’s outcome.

No offer of tokens or investments

Nothing on this site is an offer, solicitation or invitation to buy, sell or hold any token, including $XPT, or any other investment. $XPT is not listed or available. If Exe Protocol ever offers a token, it will do so only with the information and approvals of a legal professional.

Not advice

Nothing on this site is financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

Not audited

The economic model has been independently reviewed by two AI reviewers. It has not been audited. A developer review and security audit will be undertaken before any real funds are used.

Statements of intent

Some statements describe what Exe Protocol intends to build, such as finding the cheapest route, an SDK for other apps, working with x402 and other payment rails, and escrow for agent budgets. They are intentions, not commitments, and may change.

Third-party names and figures

Names such as x402, Coinbase, Cloudflare, Stripe, MetaMask, Jupiter and Hyperliquid, and the figures quoted for them, come from their public sources, linked where used. They are used for comparison only and imply no partnership or endorsement. Token icons are from the openly licensed cryptocurrency-icons set; the tokens’ names and marks belong to their owners.

Cryptoassets are high risk

Cryptoassets can lose all their value, are often not protected by compensation schemes, and can be hard to sell. Never commit money you cannot afford to lose.
